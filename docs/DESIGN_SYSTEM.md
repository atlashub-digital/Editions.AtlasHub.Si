# AtlasHub Editions — Design System Baseline

## Principle

**80% editorial luxury · 20% technology**

## Color

- Midnight: `#040914`
- Atlas Navy: `#071425`
- Atlas Electric Blue: `#147dff`
- Signal Cyan: `#55c7ff`
- Ice White: `#f5f9ff`
- Reading Paper: `#f5f2ea`
- Steel: `#8ea0b5`

## Surfaces

- Catalogue / institutional: dark
- Long-form reading: light by default
- Frameworks: can invert to dark when emphasis is needed

## Typography

The foundation uses system serif/sans stacks to avoid licensing and loading decisions during mockup. Production should select licensed, embedded editorial typefaces while preserving:
- serif for reading and intellectual hierarchy;
- sans for metadata, navigation and operational diagrams.

## Visual rules

- blue is semantic signal, not wallpaper;
- no generic SaaS dashboard aesthetic;
- no gratuitous holograms or humanoid robots;
- generous negative space;
- strong publication codes and metadata;
- publication covers are objects, not flat thumbnails;
- long-form reading prioritizes comfort over brand spectacle.
