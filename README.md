## Purpose

`editions.atlashub.si` is the canonical editorial and publishing surface of the AtlasHub ecosystem.

AtlasHub Editions is designed as a premium digital publishing house — not a generic blog and not a PDF directory.

### Publication families

- **Books** — long-form flagship works
- **eBooks** — focused digital publications
- **Executive Papers** — concise decision-oriented intelligence
- **Research** — reports and research notes
- **Field Guides** — operational frameworks and playbooks
- **Essays** — authored perspectives and long-form editorial work

## Flagship publication

**EA–001 · EMPRESA AUMENTADA**  
*Da Inteligência Artificial à Organização Inteligente*  
Sérgio Monteiro · 1.ª edição · Outubro 2026

Canonical route:

```text
editions.atlashub.si/livros/empresa-aumentada
```

Edition/archive metadata should not be embedded in the canonical URL. Historical editions may later use:

```text
/livros/empresa-aumentada/edicoes/2026-10
```

## Development workflow

1. **Phase 1 — NexFlowX-Hub:** design, engineering and editorial validation.
2. **Phase 2 — AtlasHub upstream:** reviewed release promoted to `atlashub-digital/Editions.AtlasHub.Si`.

## Local development

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open `http://localhost:3000`.

## Design direction

**80% editorial luxury · 20% technology**

- dark premium institutional surfaces;
- light reading surfaces for long-form comfort;
- restrained electric blue as semantic signal;
- strong typography and negative space;
- no generic SaaS cards or gratuitous AI imagery.
